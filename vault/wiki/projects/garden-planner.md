# Bed Map

Bed Map is Sam's fictional hobby app for sketching container beds. It is not published and it has no account system. The first version is a grid on one device.

## Goal

Draw this season's pots and boxes, then look up what was planted there last year. Sharing and sync can wait.

## Decisions

- Bed maps stay on the device. Offline is the whole first version. Sync is a later project, not part of the sketch from [[inbox/2026-03-02-raised-bed-sketch|the raised-bed capture]].
- A bed is a rectangle with a name, a sun note, and a list of crops. No photos in v0.
- Crop names link to [[wiki/topics/seasonal-sowing|seasonal sowing]] notes. They are not a catalog Sam has to maintain up front.

## Open questions

- Whether a bed remembers last year's crop, or whether that lives on the season note.
- How to export a map if the device is replaced. Not decided. Do not invent a cloud account for it.
