# Home lab

A fictional practice lab on a shelf: one small computer and one small managed switch. Nothing here is production, and the addresses are documentation ranges from the sample notes.

## Goal

Practice labeling ports and writing down what each box is for, so future Sam does not have to guess.

## Decisions

- The switch is written down as 192.0.2.10. The practice computer is 198.51.100.24. Both are documentation addresses, not a real LAN plan.
- Names in notes stay on example.com, such as lab.example.com. Do not record a private overlay hostname.
- The lab does not store recordings from [[wiki/projects/shop-notes|Shop Notes]]. Audio stays with the podcast project.

## Open questions

- Whether the garden app's future export should ever touch this lab. Not decided. See [[wiki/projects/garden-planner|Bed Map]].
