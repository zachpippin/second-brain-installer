# Shop Notes

Shop Notes is a fictional one-person podcast. Sam records it at the bench, edits it on the same computer, and keeps the files locally. There is no network, sponsor, or guest list in this template.

## Goal

Short episodes about a single shop problem: a joint, a finish, a tool that misbehaved. One idea per episode.

## Decisions

- Episode 3 is "The bench hook", outlined in [[inbox/2026-03-18-episode-outline|the inbox outline]]. It explains a hook Sam uses while cutting.
- Show notes live in the wiki, not in a separate site. See [[wiki/topics/show-notes|show notes]].
- No background music until Sam can point at a recording Sam made. Do not add a library or a license Sam does not have.

## Open questions

- Episode length. The outline says ten minutes. Sam has not timed a read-through.
