# Maple workbench

A fictional bench Sam is building from maple. It is the surface for the podcast and the place the garden trays get potted up when the weather is bad.

## Goal

A heavy bench with a row of square dog holes and a detachable hook for crosscutting.

## Decisions

- Joinery is hand-cut. See [[wiki/topics/hand-tool-joinery|hand-tool joinery]].
- The top gets a thin oil finish, not a film finish. See [[wiki/topics/oil-finishes|oil finishes]].
- The bench hook for episode 3 of [[wiki/projects/shop-notes|Shop Notes]] is a scrap project, not part of the bench itself.
