# Seasonal sowing

A fictional calendar for Sam's containers. Months are the northern-hemisphere sketch Sam wrote, not local advice for a real address.

## Summary

Greens go in early. Tomatoes wait until nights stay mild. Beans follow the greens in the same box if the greens are finished.

## Notes

- Bed Map should store a crop's season as text on the bed, not as a separate database.
- Sam has not decided whether last year's crop is a property of the bed or of the year. See the open question on [[wiki/projects/garden-planner|Bed Map]].
