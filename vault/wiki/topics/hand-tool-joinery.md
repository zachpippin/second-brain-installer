# Hand-tool joinery

Notes Sam keeps while cutting joints by hand for the [[wiki/projects/maple-workbench|maple workbench]]. This is practice, not a class.

## Summary

Square the stock, knife the lines, saw to the line, and pare the rest. Sam would rather leave a joint a little proud and plane it flush than try to hide a gap with glue.

## Notes

- Mortises are chopped from both faces. Sam marks the show face so the clean side lands up.
- A bench hook holds the piece for short cuts. The podcast outline uses that hook as the whole episode.
