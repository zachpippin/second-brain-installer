# Oil finishes

How Sam plans to finish the [[wiki/projects/maple-workbench|bench top]]. Fictional shop notes, not a product review.

## Summary

Wipe on a thin oil, let it soak, wipe it off. Several thin coats. No film finish on the top, because Sam wants to repair scratches by wiping again.

## Notes

- The first coat goes on before the dog holes get their final chamfer, so the chamfer does not drink the whole coat.
- Rags are dried flat outside the shop. Sam wrote that down after almost leaving a wad in a jar.
